# DILL AUR CHILL · Disc 01

**Sunlight to Starlight** — a 197-track, nine-chapter Desi-meets-global record for two.

**Listen:** https://dylansali.github.io/dill-aur-chill/

Forgotten 2010s Bollywood, Indian and Pakistani indie, Punjabi pop, Coke Studio, and late-night R&B, sequenced like an album that follows the sky:

| Side | Sky | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| A | Hazy sun | The First Pour · The Nostalgia Hit · The Indie Corner |
| A | Rain | Barsaat · Dil Ki Baatein |
| B | Night | The Punjabi Switch · The Singalong |
| B | Moon | After Hours |
| B | Stars | The Last Song |

## What the site does
- A live sky on the cover: hazy sun by day, moon and stars at night (follows your clock), or pick Sun / Rain / Moon / Stars yourself. "Songs for this sky" filters the disc to match.
- Search and filter by language, decade, mood, chapter, and Familiar / Forgotten / Hidden gem.
- Favourites, personal notes, pinned YouTube links, reorder, move between chapters, remove and restore, add your own songs.
- Export / import your version as JSON. Everything is saved in your browser (localStorage).
- Songs open on YouTube in a new tab. Nothing plays inside the page.

One self-contained `index.html`: no build step, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.
